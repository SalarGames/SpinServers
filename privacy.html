Privacy Policy for Spin World Fight

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Salar Games ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Spin World Fight (the "App"), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The App has no chat and no user-generated content other than your username. The information described below is collected.

a) Username (Game Tag)

When you first launch the App, you choose a unique in-game username ("Game Tag"). No password or email is required. Your username is stored on our servers so that it stays unique and so that you can play online. Your username, selected team, and avatar are visible to other players during online matches. Usernames are automatically checked by a filter, and inappropriate or offensive names are rejected.

b) Gameplay Data

To run online multiplayer, we process match data such as in-game inputs, match results, and room codes. Game progress (for example tournament progress, trophies, diamonds, and unlocked items) is stored locally on your device.

c) Technical and Connection Data

When you play online, our game servers (hosted in several regions) process your IP address and connection data in order to connect you to matches. We may also receive limited technical data such as crash reports, device type, and OS version to maintain and improve app stability.

d) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, time zone)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • App usage, diagnostics and interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and the Google AdMob Privacy & Security policies.

e) In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (e.g., diamonds, items) processed through Google Play Billing and Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not directly collect or store any payment card information. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play / Apple App Store, and their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • Apple Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide online multiplayer, match players together, and keep usernames unique
  • Prevent abuse and block inappropriate usernames
  • Display and personalize advertisements
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — payment processing

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties for more details on how they handle your information.

4. Advertising Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time where the App offers this option, or through your device settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Please note that real-time gameplay traffic between the App and our game servers is not end-to-end encrypted. We never transmit payment card details or passwords through our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, or via Google Ads Settings).
  • You can request deletion of your username and related data as described in the next section.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your username (Game Tag) and all related data stored on our servers at any time:

  • Send an email to SpinWorldFight@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  • Include your Game Tag in the message so we can find your record.
  • We will delete your data and confirm by email within 30 days.

You can also request deletion of only some of your data (for example gameplay data) without deleting your username by stating this in your email.

Progress stored locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Data held by Google (AdMob) or Apple/Google Play (purchases) is managed by them under their own policies.

9. Data Retention

We keep your username and related data for as long as your username is active, or until you request deletion. Other data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: SpinWorldFight@gmail.com

This policy applies specifically to the Spin World Fight mobile application.
