Privacy Policy for Spin World Fight

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Salar Games ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Spin World Fight (the "App"), available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration to play the App. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically:

a) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, time zone)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and the Google AdMob Privacy & Security policies.

b) In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (e.g., diamonds, items) processed through Google Play Billing and Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not directly collect or store any payment card information. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play / Apple App Store, and their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • Apple Privacy Policy

c) Device and Diagnostic Data

We may receive limited technical data (such as crash reports, device type, OS version) to help us maintain and improve app stability, either directly or through analytics/crash-reporting SDKs integrated into the App.

2. How We Use Information

Any information collected as described above is used to:

  • Display and personalize advertisements
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — payment processing
  • Google Play Games Services (if applicable) — online multiplayer / leaderboards

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties for more details on how they handle your information.

4. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the App is configured to serve a general audience, ad personalization may be limited or disabled for users identified as children in accordance with Google Play Families Policy and Apple's guidelines, where applicable.

5. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, or via Google Ads Settings).
  • You can request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us at the email below.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
